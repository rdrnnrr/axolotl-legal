# Axolotl privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy covers the Axolotl app (Fire TV, Android TV, Google TV and Android phones) and the Axolotl channel for Roku.

## What we collect
We do not run an account system and we do not operate servers that receive your viewing, search or usage data. We do not sell personal information, show advertising or use analytics trackers in the app.

## What stays on your device
Your settings, favorites, Watch Later list, watch history and saved playback positions are stored on your own device. The app can keep a backup copy of this data in your device's storage so it comes back if you reinstall the app. We do not receive that backup.

## Services the app talks to
To work, the app contacts third-party services directly from your device. They receive what any request to them includes (such as your IP address and what you look up), and their own privacy policies apply:
- movie and show information and artwork (such as TMDB and IMDb);
- trailers (YouTube);
- Cloudflare WARP, a VPN the app can use to route its streaming traffic;
- Amazon, for the optional VIP subscription (billing is handled entirely by Amazon; we never see your payment details);
- optional third-party search sources and add-ons that you can switch on in Settings. They are off by default.

## Casting and the Roku channel
When you cast, the Axolotl app on your phone or TV serves the video on your home network to the TV you chose. The Roku channel listens for commands from the Axolotl app on your local network. It has no accounts, collects nothing and does not accept connections from the internet.

## Children
Axolotl is intended for adults (18+).
